# Week 7 Subject: How to overcome yourself

## Lesson 1 Resentment and unforgiveness

> Read Matthew 6:14-15

This week, we will take a spiritual X-ray. It is easy to see flaws in others and correct someone else, but it is very difficult to see faults in yourself and deal with them. We just read about unforgiveness. Here is a dependency that Jesus personally showed us (fill in the missing words from Matthew 6:14-15):

If we ____________________________,
we will be ____________________________.
If we do not _______________________,
we will <u>not</u> be __________________________.

Unforgiveness is when we cannot forget something bad someone has done to us.
Bitterness is our attitude toward a person whom we cannot forgive.

Unforgiveness is related to a particular event, and bitterness is an attitude that has developed as a result of unforgiveness.
These two sins enslave and entangle people.

image: img_cover_01.png

If a person <u>does not forgive</u>, that person lives in constant <u>dependence</u> on the offender. Break these chains of unforgiveness in the name of Jesus! Jesus gives you the power to forgive because He forgives you.

What does it mean to forgive? It means to erase the event from your memory, stop bringing it up, and love your offender! It can be accomplished only through prayer and faith in the power of the Blood of Jesus. You can not do it yourself. Do not even try. Jesus will help you because He forgives and gives you the power to forgive.

## Lesson 2 Addictions and strongholds

> Read 2 Corinthians 10:4-5


What else does our spiritual X-ray show? Addictions are often deeply entrenched in the corners of our hearts. What are they? Things that influence, direct, and control our lives. An addiction has a strong, enthralling attraction that our mind is unable to control. This attraction is often much more powerful than our willpower.

A stronghold is something that sits like a thorn in our minds and hinders normal Christian life.

Throughout our entire lives, we carry all our bitterness, fear, and addictions with us. Years go by, and this burden gets very heavy. So many people have collapsed under the pressure of their burden and never set themselves free. Jesus wants to set you free! You do not have to carry all this garbage with you! Pray the prayer of repentance with your mentor, believing in the power of the Blood of Jesus, which sets you free.

Once, a young woman came to me and said that she was addicted to theft. She would frequently take things that did not belong to her, not shying away from taking money. This addiction impoverished her. The more she stole, the poorer she became. We prayed the prayer of repentance together. Soon after, she got a good job, and her life changed completely.
In John 8:32, the Bible says: <b>“Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free,”</b> adding in verse 36: <b>“So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.”</b>
Jesus Christ can set you free through His Word. Receive this freedom with faith. Be free!

They are being replaced with spirits, djinns, vampires, monsters, and other evil. In almost every newspaper, there is a horoscope that diminishes humans, equating them to a “pig,’’” goat,” “lobster,” and other reptiles and hooved ones.

Occultism is servitude to demons (servants of Satan) in any area of life.

There is a constant flow of TV, newspaper, and radio advertisements about “miracle makers” who say that they can heal any disease, alcoholism, and smoking in 5 minutes. You often find adverts like this one on streetlights:

## Lesson 4 Idols

> Read 1 John 5:21


What do you imagine when you hear the word “idol”? The dictionary says an idol is a statue or object worshipped like a deity. The first of the Ten Commandments says, <i>“You shall have no other gods before me. You shall not make for yourself an image in the form of anything in heaven above or on the earth beneath or in the waters below. You shall not bow down to them or worship them; for I, the Lord your God, am a jealous God, punishing the children for the sin of the parents to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me…”</i> (Exodus, 20:3-5).

Therefore, God has to be in the first place. He will never relinquish His first place or agree to be second. An idol is something that is in the first place in your life. An idol can be your child, job, car, house, garden, TV, hobby, life goal, money, religion, drugs, alcohol, illegal sexual relationships, etc. 	An idol is an addiction, and the person becomes its slave. An idol always destroys a person’s life because it gets the first place (authority).

image: img_cover_01.png

Once, a friend of mine decided to get married. We were happy for him, but with time, we noticed that his love was strange. He told everyone that he loved his wife more than life and that if she died, he would die, too. Then came wild jealousy. He would do everything to not let anyone near her. For this man, his wife gradually became an idol. He became a slave to his wife. She noticed his attitude and began manipulating him. This man took anything his wife did as divine. Her body, voice, and skills became the meaning of his life. Once, they quarreled. He lost control, and his wife called the police. Suddenly, he realized she was not a goddess but a woman with flaws. It was a severe shock for him, and he ended up in a mental hospital. What a shameful end of his love for the wife-idol. Every idol will lead to a similar result because idols destroy our lives.

## Lesson 5 Changing your lifestyle

> Read Ephesians 2:1-5

For our entire lives, we are trying to make other people love us. We often build our lives based on what relatives, fashion, or idols say. In Eph. 2:1, we read that all people live by either God’s will or the Devil’s. You may say, “I have never obeyed the Devil.” Maybe, but this world’s <u>system of moral values</u> is from the Devil. What is a system of moral values? It is a system that defines what is good and what is bad. You must desire to change this Devilish worldly system for God’s system. This is so important! Today, it has become the norm to swear at each other. People openly boast about betraying each other or their sexual depravities. People have no shame in the way they dress; nudist beaches are open; children are sold alcohol and cigarettes; multiple websites offer pornography; drugs have become a fashion. Such is the system of moral values that is dictated to this world by the Devil!

But God defined His system of moral values, which gives life.
