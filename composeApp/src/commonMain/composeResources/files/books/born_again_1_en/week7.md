# Week 7 Subject: How to overcome yourself

## Lesson 1 Resentment and unforgiveness

> Read Matthew 6:14-15

Forgiveness is essential for a Christian life.

Unforgiveness:
- destroys relationships  
- blocks spiritual growth  

We must forgive:
- quickly  
- sincerely  

---

## Lesson 2 Addictions and strongholds

> Read John 8:36

“So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.”

Addictions can control a person.

Jesus gives freedom:
- from habits  
- from bondage  
- from sin  

---

## Lesson 3 Curses and occultism

> Read Galatians 3:13

“Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law…”

Jesus has already broken every curse.

We should:
- reject occult practices  
- trust God fully  

---

## Lesson 4 Idols

> Read Exodus 20:3

“You shall have no other gods before me.”

Anything that replaces God becomes an idol:
- money  
- people  
- success  

God must be first.

---

## Lesson 5 Changing your lifestyle

> Read Romans 12:2

“Be transformed by the renewing of your mind.”

Change begins inside.

We need:
- new thinking  
- new habits  
- new priorities
