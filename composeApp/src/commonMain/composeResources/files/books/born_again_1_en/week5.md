# Week 5 Subject: Prayer is great!

## Lesson 1 What is prayer?

> Read Matthew 6:9-13

Prayer is communication with God.

It is not:
- a ritual  
- a formula  

It is:
- a relationship  
- a conversation  

God is our Father, and we can talk to Him freely.

---

## Lesson 2 How to pray every day

> Read Luke 18:1

“Always pray and not give up.”

Prayer should be:
- regular  
- sincere  
- personal  

It is better to pray every day a little than rarely for a long time.

Consistency builds relationship.

---

## Lesson 3 How to organize your prayer

> Read Mark 1:35

Jesus often prayed early in the morning.

To organize prayer:
- choose a time  
- choose a place  
- avoid distractions  

Prayer becomes stronger when it becomes a habit.

---

## Lesson 4 Types of prayer

> Read 1 Timothy 2:1

There are different types of prayer:
- thanksgiving  
- worship  
- request  
- intercession  

Prayer is not only asking — it is also thanking and praising.

---

## Lesson 5 God answers prayers

> Read Matthew 7:7

“Ask and it will be given to you…”

God answers prayers:
- sometimes immediately  
- sometimes later  
- sometimes differently  

But He always answers.

Trust Him.
