# Week 5 Subject: Prayer is great!

## Lesson 1 What is prayer?

> Read Mathew 6:6

Today, we have a new subject: prayer. What is prayer?
Prayer is a <u>conversation</u> with God. When I was a child, I did not like to pray because I did not understand it. I thought it was some ritual where one has to speak odd religious words at a specific time and place.
I thank God that He revealed the true meaning and beauty of prayer to me. Now, prayer is my favorite activity, and it is beautiful.
Once, the disciples of Jesus came to him and asked, “Teach us how to pray.”

center: <b>Let us define some</b>
center: <b>basic principles of prayer</b>

1. Prayer is personal and individual.
2. You need to learn how to pray.
3. God answers prayers!

Let us review these three principles one by one.

Prayer (conversation) should be personal and come from the bottom of your heart. You can not copy prayer. Prayer should be absolutely genuine because God knows all our thoughts. God will not accept an embellished prayer that does not come from the bottom of one’s heart. You need to pray as you talk with someone you love (your mother, father, husband, or wife). You need to open your heart in prayer and hide nothing. God will hear such a prayer!

The disciples of Jesus learned how to pray, and we have to learn it too. How could we learn to pray? Practice prayer daily, learn from your mentor or the pastor of your home church, and learn from the Bible and other useful books. SINCERITY, PERSEVERANCE, AND TIME WILL CERTAINLY PRODUCE A GOOD RESULT!
The most important thing is that prayer is not a ritual. God participates in prayer and answers them. The Bible teaches us that we can ask God for anything according to his will, and he shall give it to us. Wow! Do you know where prayer originated from? It came from the Garden of Eden, where God talked with people face-to-face before the Fall. It was the first prayer!
Prayer is a conversation between a person and God!
Do not forget that although you are the one who needs prayer, God also wants to speak with you, just like a parent enjoys a conversation with their child.

## Lesson 2 How to pray every day

>Read Colossians 4:2

In this passage, Apostle Paul says that we have to devote ourselves to prayer watchfully.

center: <b>How long do we need to pray, and when?</b>
1. Devote your best time to prayer. Do not follow the principle: “I give God what I don’t need.”
2. Train yourself to be steady and value prayer above all else in the world. It is a holy and inviolable time.

center: <b>How to pray every day?</b>
1. <u>Morning prayer</u>. After you finish your morning routines, pray a prayer of blessing for the day before you go to work.
2. <u>Before each meal</u>. A short prayer of blessing for the food.
3. <u>Evening prayer</u>. As a family, we thank God for the day and ask for His blessing on the night.
4. <u>During the day</u>. Requests and gratitude regarding various circumstances.

“But I have so little time,” you may say, “There is no comfortable place, and there are unbelievers all around me. What to do?”
Once, I was teaching my children to tidy up their room. The most important thing is to put everything in its place. If toys, clothes, and books are piled up in the middle of the room, it is not possible to find anything, and there is always a lack of space. Therefore, you need to put away things that you do not need. Recently, we cleaned our house and collected half a bag of useless things! We discovered so much free space!
Take a look at your time and think about how you spend it. Put everything in its place. Every task and activity should have its time and place.

## Lesson 3 How to organize your prayer

> Read Mathew 6:9-13

We just read “Our Father,” the most beautiful prayer. We can organize our prayer following the model of this prayer. I can also share my experience in prayer with you. For me, my morning prayer is the most important one. Let us take a look:
1. Giving thanks to God (for food, health, life, etc);
2. Praise God (exalt Him as the King, give Him all the honors);
3. Your needs (relevant, those you need answered today);
4. Repentance (from all your evil thoughts and deeds);
5. Protection for you, your family, and your home from the Devil (pray with faith and with the power of the Blood of Jesus);
6. Praise God (for hearing your prayer, as the Lord and King)

center: <b>How to learn to pray practically?</b>

1.	Write down the six points mentioned above on a separate sheet of paper or in your prayer journal.
2.	Write down your needs.
3.	Determine a period of time for every point. You can start with one minute for each point (it will add up to 6 minutes total)
4.	Take your watch with you and practice praying for one minute on each point until it becomes easy for you to pray.
5.	After a month (can be earlier or later), add one more minute for each point, and so on and so forth.

I would like to share with you a principle that is the most significant accomplishment of my whole life.
<b>If you learn to pray, you will learn how to do anything. If you do not learn to pray, you will never learn anything.</b>

Prayer is your connection with God, a contact with him, and a channel through which  he gives us power, the power that can change everything. Prayer is everything. It is not possible for you to love God and not love prayer.

In the end, I will say this: “The Devil will find a hundred reasons to stop you from praying. You need to fight yourself, your laziness, time, and circumstances for your prayer. If you put God and prayer first, you will win; if not, you will lose. In this case, the winnings are your life.”

## Lesson 4 Types of prayer

> Read Ephesians 6:18

This passage says that we are to pray with all kinds of prayers. During the previous lesson, we considered personal daily prayer, which is the most important one. What are the other types of prayer?

<b>1. Church-wide prayer.</b>
   In a church or a home church, all people pray together. What do we need to pray for? A pastor or another minister invites us to pray for some need or cause. Be attentive and follow the service. Such prayers lead to great results, so participate in them with joy.

<b>2. Prayer for the Sunday service.</b>
   There are different methods for this kind of prayer. Sometimes, a particular time can be set, for instance, on Saturdays from 10 to 11 PM. During this time, the whole church should pray. Within each home church, this hour is divided into 10 to 15-minute intervals, and 2 or 3 people are appointed for each interval. This way, the entire church is praying. There are other methods for this prayer: a 24-hour prayer, which starts on Saturday noon and continues until the service begins, praying one hour before the service, etc.

<b>3. Church-wide fasting and prayer</b>
   Your home church is assigned one day each month.  Strategic needs are set. For example, it can be a church building construction project, evangelism ministry in a particular city, etc. I would like to mention that a fast (abstention from food) only matters if accompanied by prayer. Without prayer, you are just starving yourself. How do you organize fasting and prayer? The fasting begins with the whole home church or 2 to 3 people discussing the needs and praying for them for 20 to 30 minutes. During the day, every home church member prays for those needs for 3 to 5 minutes at the start of every hour. The fasting and prayer ends with the entire home church giving thanks.

<b>4. Prayer before a meal</b>
   It is a very short prayer, for example, “Dear Jesus, we thank you for this food and ask you to bless it. Amen”.

<b>5. Prayer in various circumstances.</b>
   (At work, in a car, in any situation).
   When a difficult problem arises, you can pray, “Heavenly Father, help me. I ask you in the name of Jesus Christ”.
   If the Devil attacks and tempts you, pray: “Dear Jesus, I can do nothing without you, but you are able. Devil, go away in the name of Jesus!”
   Visible divine protection, pray: “Father, I thank you for your help…”

<b>6.</b> There are also other kinds of prayers; one of them is <b>prayer in the Holy Spirit</b>. We’ll talk about it later.
   Say “amen” at the end of every prayer. This word means “I agree, I confirm, it is true”.

I have given you these prayers as examples. So go and fill your life with prayer, and your life will be filled with God!

## Lesson 5 God answers prayers

> Read Luke 5:15-16

For a long time, I could not understand why Jesus had performed so many miracles, why Apostles Paul and Peter performed them, but I can not.
Then, I realized that no one can perform miracles except God. Prayer is a thin channel by which God’s power is given to a human, and this power performs miracles! If there is no channel or the channel is blocked, there is no power. If the channel is strong and steady, there is a lot of power.
Therefore, how could Jesus perform so many miracles and signs? He prayed a lot. If even Jesus needed prayer, then so do we!
Healing the sick, blind, and deaf, calming the storm, multiplying the bread, and resurrecting the dead are the results of prayer!
When I was 17, I started experiencing severe pains in my stomach. I was not able to eat anything because food caused extreme pain. I was examined and treated for a long time. My conditions grew worse, and I became as thin as a rake. I was deferred from military service due to my health condition. All I could eat was light soup.
One night, I sat in my room, crying. I ate a small piece of meat and was paying dearly for it. Suddenly, I remembered prayer. I kneeled and asked God to heal me. In my spirit, God revealed to me that I need to believe, and I said, “Lord, I believe that You are healing me.” I did not feel anything special at the moment, but I believed that my prayer was heard. I took a piece of meat and began to eat it, washing it down with water. It was extremely dangerous, but I believed. There was no pain! Nothing bad happened!
Sometime later, I went to take another examination and treatment. The doctor who examined me was astonished! He said, exactly: “What a fool has diagnosed you with this? You are 100% healthy”.
That is what prayer can do.

Let us summarize what we learned about prayer:

1. Prayer to God should be the highest priority.
2. When you pray, you must believe and forgive everyone.
3. If you pray constantly, you will learn how much you need to pray for a particular need and when your prayer is heard.

God will answer your prayers, too! Love prayer and pray. When the world-renowned evangelist Billy Graham was asked about the secret of his success, he said:
“There are three secrets to my ministry.

<b>THE FIRST ONE IS PRAYER,</b>
<b>THE SECOND IS PRAYER,</b>
<b>THE THIRD IS PRAYER”.</b>

I want to remind you that even Jesus needed prayer. Whoever you are, there is only one channel between Heaven and Earth: PRAYER.
