# Week 9 Subject: A room of fellowship with God

## Lesson 1 Can I hear God’s voice?

> Read Jn. 10:1-6

When Jesus was on Earth, He said He is the Shepherd, and His sheep hear and know His voice. With time, people changed this truth and started to babble about “holy” priests and “sinful” parishioners. Many began to think they had no right to speak with God because this right belonged exclusively to priests. Many myths about mediators and intercessors between God and people were created. But the Bible says we have only one Mediator and Intercessor, Jesus Christ! (1 Tim. 2:5) Jesus also said we became His children. For example, there are no mediators between me and my children. I speak with them myself and receive great joy from it.

center: <b>What does one need to hear God’s voice?</b>

1. to accept Jesus Christ as your Lord (King, Father, Master).
2. to receive forgiveness by faith through the Blood of Jesus Christ.
3. to receive baptism and be filled with the Holy Spirit.
4. to be born again (change one’s worldly lifestyle).
5. to have a consistent prayer life.

I must tell you that you have already heard God’s voice when God encouraged you to repent.

Let us look at three questions together:

center: <b>First, why do I need to hear God’s voice?</b>

Unequivocally, to fulfill His will. Through our relationship with Him, we receive comfort, encouragement, and direction for the work in His field. If you want to communicate with God for the sake of entertainment or out of curiosity, it will not work.

center: <b>Second: what in me communicates with God?</b>

When God comes into our hearts, our spirit is renewed, and it is through the spirit that fellowship with God takes place. Our soul (mind, will, memory, emotions) is directly connected to the spirit. Thus, we can respond to God through enlightenment (memory), feelings (joy, peace), will (desire), or mind (clear thoughts inside us).

center: <b>Third: when can God speak to us?</b>

I want to assure you that God speaks to us all the time, 24/7. The real question is, when are we listening to Him?

## Lesson 2 What is fellowship?

> Read Matt. 6:6.

I want to remind you again of the seriousness of a relationship with God. After frequently talking with people and looking at their problems, I discovered one thing many have in common: <u>they do not have a special time and place for fellowship with God</u>. Please pay attention to the following paragraphs! Many say they pray at the train station, others during their commute, and others whenever they have an opportunity. Some bold people say, “I pray all the time, all day, this is why I don’t have a special time and place.” Perhaps it is true, but in reality, they never pray.
You must have your own “fellowship room” with God. It does not necessarily have to be an actual room. Jesus prayed in a garden, on a hill, in a desert, and almost always at night. You can also have such a place or places (room, park, bathroom, forest, etc). I want to emphasize that time and place are not the key factors. What is essential is secrecy and regularity. Nothing and no one should hinder you. In Matthew 6:6, Jesus said we must talk with the Father in secret. Not only should no one hinder you, but you should also not be a hindrance to others. I want to warn wives and husbands: do not go to prayer right after your spouse comes from work or when you are going to sleep. God does not want to steal time from your husband, wife, or your boss at work. God wants to have His personal time with you. Find it, plan your sleep, rest, and time when you watch TV.
When I decided to speak with God daily, an attack of the Devil befell me. As soon as I entered the room, a neighbor would come to ask for a shovel, guests would arrive, or some urgent business would come up. Then, my wife and I decided that she would not call for me unless I came out myself. All temptations stopped right away.
I, along with some other pastors from Ukraine, once had the honor of attending a reception of the Prime Minister of Norway. We came to the palace right on time. We passed the guards and entered a beautiful hall. The meeting lasted for about an hour and ended with a joint prayer. I always greatly appreciated this meeting, as I understood that such a busy and important person gave us attention. But how much more do we need to value the meeting with the King of Kings, Lord of Lords, the Master of the Universe! Your prayers will pass through the ranks of angels, and you will have the great honor of speaking with the Creator of Heaven and Earth!
A room of fellowship with God is a lifestyle; it is a center and a foundation from which your new life begins.

center: The center has to be at the center!

## Lesson 3 How to hear God

> Read 2 Cor. 13:13

Apostle Paul wrote to the Corinthians and all of us that we communicate with God’s third personality: the Holy Spirit.

Here is what my fellowship with God looks like:
1. I thank and praise God.
2. I read aloud a chapter or a part of a chapter from Psalms.
3. I confess and repent of my sins before God.
4. I seek God with all my heart, and when I feel His presence, I stop talking and ask the Holy Spirit to speak.
5. God speaks to me from the inside (as if through an internal telephone).
6. After this, I read a passage from the Old and New Testaments.
7. I pray for the needs of the church and for the government, starting with my street and ending with the President of my country.
8. I pray for my personal needs and my family's needs.
9. I end with thanksgiving.

God does not always start speaking right away. Sometimes, we need patience. Do not expect Him to talk with you about far galaxies or the problems of the African Zulu tribe. The conversation will resemble a conversation between a mother and a newborn. As you grow spiritually, the contents and subjects of the conversation will become more complex. But usually, God starts by saying He loves, values, and forgives you.

center: <b>How to understand that God was speaking to you?</b>

1. Thoughts come “from the inside,” not “from your head.”
2. Thoughts come by themselves, not from our efforts.
3. Thoughts have a clear and obvious meaning.
4. The style of words and sentences may differ from your manner of talk.
5. You feel God’s presence and power. Words clearly come from God.
6. Those thoughts never contradict the Bible.

Very often, I write down what I hear from God. I have a “Spiritual diary” for this.

## Lesson 4 Different methods

> Read Heb. 1:1-2

God can speak to us not only through thoughts (look at Week 9, Lesson 1). He talks in a multifaceted way. The most important thing in fellowship with God is to open your heart completely.

center: <b>What methods can be used for our conversations with God?</b>

1. <u>Through</u> prayer and listening to the inner voice (Lesson 3)
2. <u>Through a sermon</u> during the church service. When you go to a service or before the sermon starts, ask God to speak to you personally through the Word you will hear. Take notes using a notebook or a digital device.
3. <u>Through reading the Bible</u>. We already talked about how you need to pray before reading the Bible to be able to receive revelations from God. After reading a passage from the Scripture, consider how it relates to your life.
4. <u>Through gifts of the Spirit</u>. Sudden revelations, knowledge, or reminders are a soft voice of God. Do not neglect it.
5. <u>Through other people</u>. During conversations, you can often feel that God is speaking to you directly, and this conversation is not an accident.

Of course, God has many other methods, but it is quality that matters, not quantity.
Once, when my house was under construction, and I was laying bricks while standing on scaffolding, God began to reveal the Sunday sermon to me. I climbed down, washed my hands, took off dirty clothing, and went to write down the revelation. Soon after returning to work, I heard God’s voice again and returned to write down what I heard. This repeated several times. The brother helping me asked, “Why are you running back and forth?” I said nothing because it was a revelation for me from my Lord.
God will deepen and widen His relationship with you only if you will value your fellowship with Him above all. Do not be lazy, and take notes! Buy the best notebook and pen for your meetings with the Lord! Show God that fellowship with Him is your first priority. The fellowship may fizzle out or never develop if you have a negligent attitude.
I want to remind you once again that God’s revelations <u>never and in no way</u> contradict the Bible. Examine your revelations. God has no objections if you do! Also, know that no revelation gives you the right to disobey your pastor or mentor.

center: <b>The Bible is God’s ultimate revelation!</b>

## Lesson 5 Regularity and time produce results

> Read 1 John 2:12-14


Did you learn to talk on the second day after you were born? How many months did it take you to learn to walk?
Apostle John wrote that we also go through certain stages of spiritual development.

image: img_cover_01.png
image: img_cover_01.png

When a person accepts Jesus, they become a newborn. There is so much they need to learn: living according to the Word, praying, receiving water baptism, hearing God’s voice, serving with the gifts, etc. All this takes time and persistence. The time it will take for us to become a boy, a young man, and a father depends on us. It may be 1 or 2 years or dozens of years. Your fellowship with God will not be built in one or two days. It is a lifelong commitment. There will be something new every day. If you found time for fellowship yesterday but failed today, the result will be little. God wants to deal with serious people.
Several years ago, I studied English with a small group of Christians. There were different people in the group: some were diligent, and others were not. One brother was able to speak and interpret very well after a year or two, but some did not understand or speak any English even after a long time. This man practiced English daily, while other people only did it when they had an opportunity. Some people may remain spiritual babies in a year, some in 10 years. Your spiritual age is determined not by how long you have been a Christian but by your spiritual growth in the Lord.
