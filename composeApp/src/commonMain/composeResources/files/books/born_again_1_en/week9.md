# Week 9 Subject: A room of fellowship with God

## Lesson 1 Can I hear God’s voice?

> Read John 10:27

“My sheep listen to my voice; I know them, and they follow me.”

God speaks to His people.

We can hear Him:
- through the Bible  
- through the Holy Spirit  
- through circumstances  

The key is to learn to recognize His voice.

---

## Lesson 2 What is fellowship?

> Read 1 John 1:3

Fellowship is:
- relationship  
- connection  
- communication  

It is not religion — it is living interaction with God.

---

## Lesson 3 How to hear God

> Read Isaiah 30:21

“Whether you turn to the right or to the left, your ears will hear a voice…”

To hear God:
- be quiet  
- be attentive  
- spend time with Him  

God often speaks gently.

---

## Lesson 4 Different methods

> Read Hebrews 1:1-2

God speaks in different ways:
- through Scripture  
- through inner conviction  
- through other people  

We must test everything by the Bible.

---

## Lesson 5 Regularity and time produce results

> Read Jeremiah 29:13

“You will seek me and find me when you seek me with all your heart.”

Consistency is key.

The more time we spend with God:
- the clearer we hear  
- the deeper the relationship
