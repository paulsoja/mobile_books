# Week 3 Subject: The redeeming power of Jesus

## Lesson 1 Why Jesus

> Read Matthew 1:18-25

After the last week’s lessons, you may ask, “Who is Jesus anyway?” Let us talk about Jesus Christ. Jesus Christ came into this world according to Biblical prophesies.

The Bible says that He always was! Jesus is the second person of the <b>Trinity</b>: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit. They are three personalities, but simultaneously, they are one.

Christ was born to the Virgin Mary and (most important) through God the Holy Spirit! He was both God and human.

Why did Jesus become human? He did it to bring salvation to humanity. To understand an ant and help an ant, one must also become an ant!

Until Jesus turned thirty, He lived like an ordinary man, studying and perceiving human life. He helped His father, Joseph, who was a carpenter.

At the age of thirty, Jesus was baptized in water. Then He was baptized with the Holy Spirit. For three and a half years, Jesus walked on Earth, preached the Kingdom of God, healed the sick, drove out evil spirits, performed miracles, and raised the dead.

When Jesus was thirty-three and a half, He was arrested and crucified. He was beaten, mocked, and nailed to the cross. He died, but God resurrected Him.

He became <b>Jesus Christ — the Messiah</b>.

God gave Him all authority. He received power to forgive sins, heal, and judge people.

<b>Why Jesus?</b>

Because He was without sin.  
Because He died willingly.  
Because He loved so much.

Without Jesus, everyone would have died!

## Lesson 2 How to receive forgiveness

> Read 1 John 1:7-10, 2:1-2

If you acknowledge Jesus as your Lord and ask Him for forgiveness, repenting of your sins, He will forgive you.

The Bible says that we shall not sin, but if we do, we must confess our sin before God, and He will forgive us.

<b>Warning!</b> The Devil has two traps:

1) “You are too sinful — God will not forgive you.” → <b>Lie</b>
2) “Sin freely — God will forgive anyway.” → <b>Lie</b>

Beware of these traps.

How to receive forgiveness:

1. Proclaim Christ as Lord
2. Acknowledge your sin
3. Confess it
4. Receive forgiveness by faith
5. Give thanks

Example:

“Dear Jesus, I give my life to You. I repent. I confess my sin. Forgive me. I receive Your forgiveness. Thank You, Jesus. Amen.”

There is no person who does not sin. But:

<b>God forgives only those who repent.</b>

Advice:

- Ask for forgiveness immediately
- Do not hide sin
- Name your sin clearly

## Lesson 3 The power of the Blood of Christ

> Read Hebrew 9:14

In 1983, I was discharged from the army. I became a part-time student and found a job. I also worked a lot in the church as a choir leader, evangelist, and youth leader. Two years later, I started building my own house. I did not have money, so I had to do everything by myself. Sunday was my only day off, so I had to build my house during lunchtime and late at night. I would take a train to church almost every day, and I had to walk 3 kilometers to the train station. I could not get good food because all the money was used for construction. It was normal for me to fill a few cubic meters of cement or unload a truckload of sand with a shovel during my lunch break. I had worked under such pressure for five years. During that time, our evangelism team conducted about a hundred meetings in different places.

One day, I felt very bad. I had a high fever, weakness, and severe pain. I could not even stand up. I understood that I was seriously ill. I started praying and asking God for help. Suddenly, I remembered the Scripture: “The Blood of Jesus Christ cleanses us from all sin.”

At that moment, I clearly understood that the Blood of Jesus Christ has power not only to cleanse us from sin but also to heal our body and restore our strength.

I began to pray differently:

“Lord Jesus, I thank You for Your Blood. I believe that Your Blood cleanses me from all sin and heals me from all sickness.”

After this prayer, I immediately felt relief. The fever went away, the pain disappeared, and strength returned to my body. I stood up and continued working as if nothing had happened.

From that day, I understood a very important truth:

<b>The Blood of Jesus has power!</b>

What does the Blood of Jesus do?

1. The Blood of Jesus purifies from sin.
2. The Blood of Jesus gives power to overcome sin.
3. The Blood of Jesus makes us righteous before God.

The Blood of Jesus is the greatest value in the spiritual world.

God the Father responds only to the Blood of His Son.

When we come to God through the Blood of Jesus, He accepts us, forgives us, and gives us power.

Never forget:

<b>There is power in the Blood of Jesus!</b>

## Lesson 4 Always with us!

> Read Matthew 28:18-20

When I was a child, I often regretted that I was not born at the time when Jesus Christ lived on Earth. I thought that if I had lived then, I would have seen Him, heard Him, and followed Him everywhere.

But one day, I understood something very important: today, we have even more!

Before ascending to Heaven, Jesus said very important words to His disciples.

First, He said that <b>all authority in Heaven and on Earth belongs to Him</b>.

Second, He commanded His disciples to go and preach the Gospel to all people.

Third, and most important, He said:

<b>“I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”</b>

This means that Jesus did not leave us alone. He is always with us!

How is this possible?

Jesus is with us through the Holy Spirit.

When we believe in Him, accept Him as our Lord, and begin to follow Him, the Holy Spirit comes into our life.

Because of this:

* God hears our prayers
* People receive salvation
* The sick are healed
* Evil spirits leave
* Lives are changed
* Circumstances change
* Life receives meaning

All of this happens because <b>Jesus is with us</b>.

He is not far away. He is not somewhere in Heaven only.

He is <b>right here, with us</b>.

You can talk to Him.

You can ask Him for help.

You can rely on Him in any situation.

Never forget:

<b>Jesus is always with you!</b>

He never leaves you.

He never lies.

He never fails.

He is always faithful.

## Lesson 5 You can do it with Jesus!

> Read Philippians 4:12-13

“Good for you—you may say to me—you have been a believer since you were a child, you have experience, you have strength. But what about me? I am weak. I cannot do it.”

These thoughts come to many people.

But the truth is very simple:

<b>You can do everything with Jesus!</b>

The apostle Paul said:

“I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.”

It does not matter who you are:

* young or old
* strong or weak
* rich or poor
* educated or not

It does not matter what your past is.

It does not matter how many times you have failed.

With Jesus — everything is possible.

You may say:

“I cannot stop sinning.”

Yes, by yourself — you cannot.

But with Jesus — you can.

You may say:

“I cannot change my life.”

Yes, by yourself — you cannot.

But with Jesus — you can.

You may say:

“I am afraid.”

Yes, by yourself — you are.

But with Jesus — you will overcome fear.

Jesus gives strength.

Jesus gives power.

Jesus gives victory.

What should you do?

Ask Him.

In every situation:

* at school
* at work
* at home
* in difficulties
* in temptations

Pray and ask:

“Jesus, help me.”

And He will help you.

Do not be afraid if you fail.

Do not give up.

If you fall — stand up again.

And go forward.

Jesus is with you.

He will support you.

He will strengthen you.

He will lead you.

And you will win.

Never forget:

<b>You can do everything with the empowering help of Jesus Christ!</b>
