# Week 3 Subject: The redeeming power of Jesus

## Lesson 1 Why Jesus?

> Read John 14:6

Jesus said: “I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”

Many people ask: why exactly Jesus? Why not another way?

There are many religions and philosophies, but only Jesus Christ:
- lived a sinless life
- died for our sins
- rose again

He did not just teach truth — He is the Truth.

Every person has sinned and needs salvation. But no one can save themselves. Only someone without sin could take our place.

Jesus is the only one who meets this condition.

---

## Lesson 2 How to receive forgiveness

> Read 1 John 1:9

“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness.”

Forgiveness is not earned — it is received.

To receive forgiveness:
1. Recognize your sin  
2. Repent  
3. Confess it to God  
4. Accept Jesus as Lord  

God does not remember confessed sins. He cleanses us completely.

---

## Lesson 3 The power of the Blood of Christ

> Read Hebrews 9:22

“Without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.”

In the Old Testament, animals were sacrificed for sins.

But those sacrifices were temporary.

Jesus became the final sacrifice.

His blood:
- cleanses us  
- redeems us  
- gives us access to God  

It is not symbolic — it is real spiritual power.

---

## Lesson 4 Always with us!

> Read Matthew 28:20

“And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.”

Jesus is not distant.

He is always with us:
- in difficulties  
- in decisions  
- in daily life  

The Holy Spirit lives in us and guides us.

We are never alone.

---

## Lesson 5 You can do it with Jesus!

> Read Philippians 4:13

“I can do all this through him who gives me strength.”

Christian life is not based on our strength.

It is based on:
- God’s power  
- God’s grace  
- God’s presence  

With Jesus:
- we overcome sin  
- we grow  
- we change  

You are not alone in this journey.
