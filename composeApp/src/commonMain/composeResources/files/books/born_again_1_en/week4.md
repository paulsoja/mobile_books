# Week 4 Subject: Church is my new family

## Lesson 1 Why go to church?

> Read Hebrews 10:25

“Not giving up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another…”

Church is not just a building.

It is:
- a spiritual family  
- a place of growth  
- a place of support  

A Christian cannot grow alone.

---

## Lesson 2 Home church is a spiritual family

> Read Acts 2:42

“They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to fellowship…”

The early church met in homes.

Home groups are important because:
- they build relationships  
- they allow deeper learning  
- they provide support  

Church is not about attendance — it is about belonging.

---

## Lesson 3 What happens in a church and why?

> Read 1 Corinthians 14:26

Church gatherings include:
- teaching  
- prayer  
- worship  
- encouragement  

Everything is done to strengthen believers.

Church helps us grow spiritually and stay on the right path.

---

## Lesson 4 Offerings and tithe

> Read Malachi 3:10

Giving is not about money — it is about the heart.

Tithe:
- is 10%  
- belongs to God  

Offerings:
- are voluntary  
- express gratitude  

Giving teaches trust and dependence on God.

---

## Lesson 5 Mentorship

> Read 2 Timothy 2:2

Mentorship is:
- learning from someone  
- growing under guidance  

Every Christian needs:
- someone to learn from  
- someone to guide  

Mentorship helps avoid mistakes and grow faster.
