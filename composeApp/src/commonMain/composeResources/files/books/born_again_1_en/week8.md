# Week 8 Subject: A serious relationship

## Lesson 1 Faithfulness

> Read Luke 16:10

“Whoever can be trusted with very little can also be trusted with much…”

Faithfulness is important in every area:
- in small things  
- in big things  

God values consistency and reliability.

---

## Lesson 2 Water baptism

> Read Matthew 28:19

Baptism is:
- a command from Jesus  
- a public declaration of faith  

It symbolizes:
- death to old life  
- new life with Christ  

---

## Lesson 3 Baptism with the Holy Spirit

> Read Acts 1:8

“You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you…”

The Holy Spirit:
- empowers  
- guides  
- strengthens  

He helps us live a Christian life.

---

## Lesson 4 Gifts of the Spirit

> Read 1 Corinthians 12:4-7

There are different spiritual gifts:
- wisdom  
- knowledge  
- faith  
- healing  

They are given to serve others.

---

## Lesson 5 Exams

> Read James 1:2-3

Trials are part of growth.

They:
- strengthen faith  
- build character  

Difficulties are not obstacles — they are opportunities.
