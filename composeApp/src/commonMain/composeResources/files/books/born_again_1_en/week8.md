# Week 8 Subject: A serious relationship

## Lesson 1 Faithfulness

> Read Mathew 10:32-33

A person’s moral qualities were always highly valued, but among them, faithfulness was especially appreciated. That’s why the name of Judas became eponymous with people’s contempt for betrayal.
The opposite of faithfulness is self-interest (if it benefits me, I do it, and if it doesn’t, I don’t).

Why is everything so serious? The Son of God paid for our salvation with His Blood, suffering, and life. Believing in Jesus is not about feelings or money. It is far more serious, an issue of life and death: I will either live forever with Jesus or suffer forever with the Devil. The Bible says, “there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.” Just like Heaven, Hell will never end. It is eternal. One preacher said, “If once every million years an eagle would have flown from the Moon to the Earth, picked up a grain of sand, and flown back, then by the time he would have moved our entire planet, eternity would have just begun.” Then what is the meaning of our life that lasts 70 or 100 years? This is why Jesus valued the salvation of people so much.

When we make any commercial deal (it may be the purchase of bread, a car, or a house), there are three things present:
1) trust;
2) contract (receipt);
3) payment.

When we accept the gift of eternal salvation from sin and punishment for sin, we also make these three steps:
1. We receive salvation by faith in Jesus Christ;
2. We make an agreement with God about salvation and faithfulness (water baptism);
3. Christ pays for our salvation Himself and gives it to us already paid for.

All over the world, serious deals are based on contracts. If a young man tells his girlfriend he loves her but refuses to marry her, he is a liar and traitor. His mind is far from love. He wants to use the girl to satisfy his lust on an animalistic level. True love is not afraid of a marriage certificate.
I knew a person who paid a high price for neglecting contracts. His relative came to this man and borrowed a significant amount of money for a short term. A while later, the man called his relative and said it was time to return the money. He pretended he did not take anything from him and had no idea what he was talking about. A contract is a very serious thing, especially if it has to do with life and death! God wants to have a serious, wonderful, and eternal relationship with you!

## Lesson 2 Water baptism

Read 1 Peter 3:21

What is water baptism? The Greek word “baptismos” means “submersion.” When a person made a serious decision to become a Christian, they would stand in water before witnesses and promise to serve God. A church minister would then immerse this person into the water, symbolizing the death of their old sinful life and resurrection for a new life.

center: <b>Why do we need</b> 
center: <b>to receive water baptism?</b>

1. Because it is the only way to be saved (Mk. 16:16)
2. Because Jesus Christ received water baptism (Mk. 1:9)
3. Because water baptism is an eternal agreement with God (Rom. 5:1)

center: <b>Who can receive water baptism?</b>

1. Any person who accepted Jesus as their Lord and repented of their sins.
2. The age of a person who can receive water baptism is determined by their ability to make decisions. Therefore, infants can not be baptized.
3. “If you believe with all your heart, you may.” the Bible says.

But when does a person need to be baptized? The next day after repentance or one year later? Does it have to happen only when one is spiritually mature and good? <u>You need to receive water baptism when you made a firm decision to follow Christ and repented from your sins</u>. My father was baptized when he was 15. I received water baptism at 19 and my daughter when she was 13. It needs to be your faith and decision.
Water baptism is always a celebration. The people who are being baptized wear white clothing and look like angels. God performs many miracles during water baptisms: people are baptized with the Holy Spirit, healed from illnesses, and released from oppression.
Millions of people were baptized in water, including Jesus Christ, the Apostles, Volodymyr the Great, our fathers and grandfathers. The list goes on and on.
I’d like to note that you need to receive water baptism not after you <u>become</u> good but when you <u>want</u> to become good. Water baptism was not always as easy to obtain as it is today. There were times when people were fined, imprisoned, or even killed for it. But no one could stop people's unwavering desire for a serious and eternal relationship with God built on trust. After baptism, you belong entirely to God and have His protection, grace, and love.

## Lesson 3 Baptism with the Holy Spirit

> Read Acts 1:8, 2:1-4

The Bible tells us about one more baptism: baptism with the Holy Spirit, and it is one of the most wonderful experiences in a person’s life. A special sign that accompanies baptism with the Holy Spirit is speaking in tongues. People respond to the baptism in different ways: some cry, some sing, and some express great joy. Sometimes, people shake because of the overflowing emotions, but these are secondary things.

center: <b>There are two key factors in baptism with the Holy Spirit:</b>

1. We receive the supernatural power to testify about Jesus Christ.
2. We receive special gifts of the Spirit: knowledge, tongues, healings, ministry, etc.

Figuratively, it can be said that water baptism makes us soldiers of Christ, and spiritual baptism equips us for battle.

center: <b>How to receive baptism</b>
center: <b>with the Holy Spirit?</b>
center: <b>Three steps to baptism</b>
center: <b>with the Holy Spirit:</b>

1. <u>Thirst</u>. The Bible says, “Let anyone who is thirsty come to me and drink.” You need to have a firm desire for the Holy Spirit.
2. <u>Request</u>. Ask in prayer because the Bible says, “Ask and it will be given to you…”
3. <u>Faith</u>. Baptism belongs to all Christians. God gives us the Holy Spirit not according to our deeds but as a gift. Receive the Holy Spirit!
I want to remind you not to control the process of the baptism with your mind. We must completely give our will, emotions, and lips to the Holy Spirit. If there is control, there will be no baptism. Only faith is needed!

center: <b>Why do we need the baptism with the Holy Spirit?</b>

1. To pray and intercede in tongues when the Holy Spirit drives us;
2. To speak with the Holy Spirit through our renewed spirit;
3. To use the gifts of the Spirit in ministry as a powerful weapon of God.
4. To, having received the power of the Spirit, bring lost people back to Christ.

As a Christian, I prayed about baptism with the Holy Spirit for a long time. I often gave way to despair, thinking I was not worthy. Once, when I was praying, I told God, “I wish I could speak in tongues.” God replied, “Speak then, you keep asking all the time and do not give any room to me, stop and speak”. Prayer began to flow from my heart like a river, and there was great joy. Once you are baptized, be filled with the Spirit every day, be eager for the Spirit, develop your relationship with the Spirit, and rivers of living water will flow from you.

## Lesson 4 Gifts of the Spirit

> Read 1 Cor. 12

If God baptized you with the Holy Spirit, you have great potential!

center: <b>First, what is a gift?</b>
Literally, it is a present with which we receive a supernatural ability for one or several types of ministries to other people in the home church (church). There are many gifts, but the main ones are listed in 1 Corinthians 12:8-10:
 - message of wisdom
 - gifts of healing
 - message of knowledge
 - miraculous powers
 - faith
 - prophesy
 - speaking in different kinds of tongues
 - interpretation of tongues

center: <b>Second, how to receive a gift?</b>

When the Holy Spirit comes upon us, the doors to gifts open before us. First, we need to be filled with the Spirit every day. Second, we need to obey God. Third, we need to believe. A gift comes progressively and needs to be developed. I want to mention that God gives us gifts Himself. We do not choose them. Do not hurry to have a gift of healing or miraculous powers. God knows the gift through which our personality will be most fruitful.

center: <b>Third, where and how to use the gifts?</b>

1. Gifts need to be used to serve other people, first and foremost, in your home church.
2. Gifts shall be used only for the improvement of the church, not for its destruction.
3. Love is the driving force behind every gift. If you are driven by anything else (proving that you’re right, humiliating someone, justifying yourself), it is not from the Lord.
I want to tell you a story about how multi-faceted the gifts of the Spirit are in action. Once, I attended a church service with our evangelism group. There was a man whose mother was very ill, and we were invited to come and pray for her. When we came into their home, we learned that she had been in bed for three days and suffered from severe headaches and heart pains. She could not get up from her bed and suffered greatly, even though an ambulance had already come to her and tried to help her as much as they could. Her husband was a deacon at a local church. We began to pray together. After a short prayer, she said her headache stopped, but her heart pains continued. She felt exhausted. Suddenly, I received a message of knowledge and said, “Her husband must pray for her.” My brothers confirmed it, as they received a similar message. We asked her husband to pray. At first, he refused, but we insisted, and he put his hands on her. Immediately, something happened, and they began to cry. Several minutes later, she was completely healthy! I did not understand what happened, but then their son, who invited us, explained that his parents quarreled bitterly, and she became very ill as a result. The moment her husband laid his hands on her, they were reconciled, and healing came to her. How wonderful is our God! Pray and be eager to have the Holy Spirit and His gifts!

## Lesson 5 Exams

> Read Luke 4:1-3

In any university, college, or school, there are exams that evaluate our knowledge and abilities. Luke writes that after the water baptism and the baptism with the Holy Spirit, the Spirit led Jesus to the wilderness to be tempted (tested).
<b>Temptation</b> is a circumstance, problem, or attack of the Devil that suddenly befalls us and our lives to test our faith and faithfulness to God.
No one graduates without exams, and there are plenty of exams during the studying itself. God can not entrust something important to us if he does not see our faithfulness. You will face tests on your journey! Satan attacks people with extraordinary ferocity before and after baptism. Problems appear at work and in the family. Suddenly, someone gets sick, or a relative visits or invites you. And all this happens precisely at the time of baptism!
Jesus Christ fought temptations with God’s pure Word. Use this method, too! Do not argue, complain, or try to prove anything. Simply proclaim God’s Word (look at Lesson 4 of Week 5).

center: <b>I want to give you some helpful advice on how to fight temptation:</b>

1. <u>Do not complain</u>. Complaining is one of the most terrible sins.
2. <u>Do not despair</u>. A test is temporary.
3. <u>Be steadfast and faithful</u> because that’s what God wants to see in you.
4. <u>Share</u> your situation with your mentor and in your home church. It is easier to overcome problems together!
5. If you want temptation to be over quickly, <u>make a firm decision</u> to remain faithful. Satan will see it and retreat, understanding that he can do nothing to you.
Once, my younger son fell and broke a finger. Then, I had to take my wife to the hospital because her pregnancy was threatened, and on our way to the hospital, my car broke down. Satan sent me thoughts: “Where is your God? Why do such things happen to a pastor? God does not hear you. No one needs you”. I prayed and stood firm in my trust in the Lord. The finger was miraculously healed, a healthy and beautiful son was born, and God gave me an opportunity to buy another car.
Be firm, and remember that the Lord will give you strength to overcome all temptations!
