# Week 6 Subject: The most interesting and useful book

## Lesson 1 Bread will never get boring

> Read Matthew 4:4

“Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that comes from the mouth of God.”

Just like the body needs food, the spirit needs God’s Word.

We eat every day, and we do not get tired of it.

In the same way, we should read the Bible daily.

---

## Lesson 2 A little bit about the Bible

> Read 2 Timothy 3:16

“All Scripture is God-breathed…”

The Bible:
- was written by different people  
- over many years  
- but has one Author — God  

It is:
- true  
- reliable  
- living  

---

## Lesson 3 How to read the Bible

> Read Psalm 119:105

“Your word is a lamp for my feet…”

To read the Bible:
- regularly  
- attentively  
- with prayer  

Ask God to reveal meaning.

---

## Lesson 4 Practical application of the Bible

> Read James 1:22

“Do not merely listen to the word… Do what it says.”

Reading is not enough.

We must:
- apply  
- live  
- obey  

Only then it transforms us.

---

## Lesson 5 Faith

> Read Romans 10:17

“Faith comes from hearing the message…”

Faith grows through:
- hearing God’s Word  
- reading Scripture  
- applying truth  

The more we engage with the Bible, the stronger our faith becomes.
