# Week 6 Subject: The most interesting and useful book

## Lesson 1 Bread will never get boring

> Read Mathew 4:4

How fast time flies! We are now in Week 6 of our lessons. I hope you became friends with your mentor and grew spiritually. This week, we will study the Bible, which is a book that is read more than any other book in the world.
We just read the words of Jesus. To live physically, we need bread (food), and to live spiritually, we need the Word of God (the Bible). Not all countries in the world have the same “bread.” In China, it is rice. In some countries, it is biscuits. In other ones, it may be fish, vegetables, or fruits. Bread is the most important and essential product. In Ukraine, for example, potatoes can also be considered “bread”.
The Bible contains many books and a great number of different stories and events. As a whole, it has been the spiritual bread for all people for thousands of years. There are a lot of good and important books, but most of them quickly get old and fade into obscurity along with their authors.

“What about the classics–you may ask–the masterpieces of world culture, textbooks, and fiction?” Well, let us put everything in its place.

1. There is the bread in the first place: the Word of God, the Bible
2. There are good dietary supplements. They are not essential but useful: good books.
3. There is corrupted and poisonous food. These are books (movies, music), social networks, and YouTube channels that contain demonism (fortune-telling, hypnosis, magic, Eastern religions), pornography, sadism, horror, etc.

Will it not get boring to always read the Bible? Several years ago, when I was very hungry, I ate too much halva. But I have never overeaten bread. One can eat too much chocolate or jam, but not too much bread!

Almost every day, we dispose of garbage from our kitchen. My wife is always careful so that nothing harmful will get into our food. Throw away all spiritual garbage and waste so that your life will not reek. Check your books and videos. Throw out, burn, and delete all the trash! Remember that you can only throw away things that belong to you. If something is not yours, discuss it with the owner.

image: img_cover_01.jpg

One more thing: you can not fill yourself with a lifetime of bread at once. You need to eat something every day. The same is true about reading the Bible. I have read the Bible cover to cover many times. I still read it daily, and the Word of God is always new, pleasant, and fresh to me. Love the Bible and read it!

## Lesson 2 A little bit about the Bible

> Read 2 Peter 1:19-21

Many authors wrote the Bible, but they all wrote under the dictation of the Holy Spirit. The Bible reflects God. In the Gospel of John, it is written that the Word (Bible) is the essence of Jesus Christ. How many Bibles are there? There is only one Bible. There are many translations into different languages. But there is only one Bible. There is no Catholic, Orthodox, or Baptist Bible. There is the Bible.
The Bible consists of 66 books and is divided into two parts:
1. The Old Testament (39 books)
2. The New Testament – the Gospel (27 books)

There might be various appendixes at the end of a Bible: commentaries, maps, and historical data. They are helpful, but they are not a part of the Bible.
Every book in the Bible is divided into chapters, and chapters are divided into verses. When the Scripture is quoted, the names of the books are often shortened:
For example, 1 Cor. 3:2 means the First Epistle of the Apostle Paul to Corinthians, Chapter 3, verse 2.
The first five books of the Bible make up the Old Covenant Laws. We do not live according to the Law but according to the Gospel. Jesus Christ fulfilled the Law in its entirety and gave us the Gospel. Today, the Old Testament sets an example for us, while the New Testament is the manual for our lives. The news about salvation for humanity through Jesus Christ is a recurring narrative in the Bible.
Sometimes, when people read the Bible, they ask, “Why are there cruel episodes of war or bad things in the lives of some people in the Bible?” The Word of God contains the history of humanity with all its sins and vices. The Bible shows everything  truthfully and without embellishment.
Here is a brief summary of the Bible:
<i>God created the world, and it was good. People disobeyed God and sinned. The Lord tried to bring humankind back to life using various methods. He revealed forgiveness through animal sacrifice to people. Many prophets were sent, but humanity delved deeper and deeper into sin. By God’s grace, Jesus Christ was born. He gave His life for people’s sins and returned to the Father. The Holy Spirit was sent to aid the Church, and now, He is guiding the Church. Jesus Christ will come for a second time to take His Church, and the rest of the world will be judged according to their deeds.</i>

## Lesson 3 How to read the Bible

> Read 1 Tim. 4:16

The Bible contains a multitude of treasures. How to extract them? I’ll share three secrets with you:

center: <b>1st secret</b>

You need to <u>read</u> the Bible, read it routinely and daily. I advise you to start by reading one or several chapters from the New Testament a day. Bread gives you strength for a day, but the next day, it weakens. The same is true with the Bible. Every day needs its own revelation from the Word.

center: <b>2nd secret</b>

Read with <u>prayer</u>. Ask God for the ability to understand what you are reading. Ask for wisdom and special revelations from the Bible. Do not forget to thank God for His Word.

center: <b>3rd secret</b>

After you read the Bible, <u>apply</u> the Word in your life. Remember, even if you learn all the wisdom of God and know all the mysteries of the world but do not apply any of it in your life, it is all in vain.

Let us open Ephesians 2:8-9. It says, <b>“For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith—and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God—not by works, so that no one can boast.”  You can also read it like this, “For it is by grace I have been saved, through faith—and this is not from myself, it is the gift of God—not by works, so that I can not boast.”</b>

I also read the Bible when I pray. I thank God and praise Him, and then I read 5-10 verses from the book of Psalms aloud. The Word guides my prayer in the right direction.

You can also study the Bible. What does it say about love, faith, eternal life, etc?
A book that lists words from the Bible in alphabetical order is a very helpful tool for quickly searching for a needed passage. There are also excellent dictionaries, reference books, and commentaries. But the Bible is the most important.
You can also get a special notebook in which you will write down the revelations you receive when you read the Bible. If you do not understand a passage, ask your mentor or the pastor of your home church. Share your revelations from the Word with your mentor and during your home church meetings.
I want to give you three pieces of advice to understand the Bible:

<b>READ!</b>
<b>READ!</b>
<b>READ!</b>

## Lesson 4 Practical application of the Bible

> Read Eph. 6:10-17

The Bible has many meanings and applications. We just read that the Word of God is like a sword. Why do we need a sword? We need it to attack. A shield is for defense. When Jesus was tempted in the desert, he overcame the Devil by simply quoting the Bible. It is a spiritual sword.

center: <b>How to practically apply the Word of God?</b>

<u>Memorize</u> the most important verses from the Bible. You may need them at any moment.

<u>Quote</u> verses aloud during moments of temptation. The Devil is afraid of the Word and your faith in it.

<u>Think and dream</u> in accordance with the Word of God. The power of the Word should renew your mind.



A sister from our church was greatly suffering from the spirit of fear. She was afraid of everything and everywhere. The fear tormented her day and night. As a result, she could not remain in her apartment alone, even during the day. There were many prayers and conversations, but nothing helped. The spirit of fear enslaved her mind. But then, God enlightened her mind through the Word, and she understood that there is no fear in God's perfect love. This understanding of His Word enlightened her mind, and she was completely free from fear.

center: <b>Some practical tips on the application of God’s Word:</b>

1. <u>In times of temptation</u> (fear, depression, bad news, despair, “nobody loves me,” “no one needs me,” etc.), apply James 4:7, <i>“Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.”</i> To submit means to listen, to resist means not to listen. Say, <i>“Lord, I obey You. You have the power. I can do nothing, but You can do everything. Devil (fear, depression, etc.), be gone in the name of Jesus!”</i> Repeat this until the temptation will go away.

2. <u>When disappointed</u> in faith (“God does not hear me,” “I am not saved,” “God will never forgive me,” etc.), quote Romans 8:38-39. Instead of the words “us” and “our,” use the words “me” and “my.” Cite this passage until complete victory.

3. <u>In times of sickness</u> (prayer for the sick, symptoms of an illness, chronic diseases, etc.), quote 1 Peter 2:24. Instead of the words “our,” “we,” and “you,” use the words “my” and “I.” It is very important to proclaim these words <u>with faith</u> because it is impossible to please God without faith.

## Lesson 5 Faith

> Read Heb. 11:6

We have already learned many new things. We understood and discovered a lot. But there is something without which all of it is in vain, and it is faith. Where does faith come from?

Read Rom. 10:17 and fill in the missing words:
<i>“Consequently, faith comes from _____________________________, and _________________________________through _________________________.”</i>

Faith comes when we perceive (hear) the Word of God. Faith is a condition without which God does not accept us or our prayers.

center: <b>Why do we need faith?</b>

1. To receive forgiveness for our sins, we need prayer with confession + faith.
2. To have the strength to overcome sin, we need to profess the power of the Blood of Jesus + our faith.
3. To receive healing from illness, we need prayer according to the Word + faith.
4. To receive the ability to live by the Word (righteousness), we need the profession and acceptance of the righteousness of Jesus + faith.

Faith is of immense importance. It is what turns theory into practice. The Bible says that the righteous shall live by <u>faith</u>.

<b>Thus, we can live by faith in:</b>
1. The Blood of Jesus (which cleanses us);
2. The holiness of Jesus (the power to win);
3. The righteousness of Jesus (the ability to live);
4. The love of Jesus (the ability to love).

Where does faith come from? It comes from the Word of God. We can <u>provisionally</u> divide God’s Word into two parts:

<b>“Rhema”</b> is what we hear (perceive);
<b>“Logos”</b> is what we read (text);

Faith comes from “rhema.” “Rhema” comes from “logos.”
In all your prayers, whatever you ask, you need to believe, believe, and believe. But it should be based on the Word only! True faith comes from the Word of God.
There is a proverb about an old woman. In the Bible, she read that if a believer tells a mountain, “Go, throw yourself into the sea,” it will be done. A mountain in front of her house bothered her very much. She prayed all night for the mountain to disappear. When she looked through the window in the morning, she saw that it was still there. She said, “I knew it. It will never move.”

One man said: "I believe in “after,” not “before.” In reality, “after” is knowledge, and “before” is faith.
It is not enough to read the Bible. You need to “hear.” And you can only “hear” if you read with prayer and a sincere and open heart.
