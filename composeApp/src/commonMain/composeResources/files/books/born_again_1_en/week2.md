# Week 2 Subject: The cause of all problems is sin

## Lesson 1 How it all happened

> Read Chapters 1-3 of the book of Genesis

Today, we begin a new topic: The cause of all problems is sin. I believe you have noticed several things after reading the first three chapters of Genesis:

1. God created everything.
2. Adam and Eve were at the center of creation.
3. The Serpent (Devil) deceived people.

Let us review, step by step.

God created everything good. He did not plan sin, disease, famine, or war. Humans were His most important creation. Everything was created for them: Earth, the Universe, plants, and animals. What made humans different from animals, in your opinion? Was it their intellect, strength, or memory? It was more than that. Humans were created in God’s image and likeness and had the right to choose.

When Adam and Eve were given a choice (God explained everything to Adam): whether to have joy, happiness, prosperity, health, and eternity in obedience to God or to be independent and face death, disease, and poverty, they chose the latter option of independence. And along with that independence, they got all the problems we experience now. When I was a child, I used to think that if I were Adam, I would have chosen God. No. In the position of Adam and Eve, we all would have done the same.

There is an old story about a newlywed couple and a rich man. The couple grumbled about Adam and Eve. They said their lives would have been different if Adam and Eve had not done what they did. One rich Christian heard about this and met this newly married couple. He said he would provide them with everything they needed: a house, furniture, and money, but under one condition. There will be two plates on their table, one flipped on top of the other, and they are not allowed to open them. If they do it even once, they will lose everything they received from him. They were patient for a couple of days, but then, when the husband was not at home, the wife lifted the top plate a little, and suddenly, a mouse jumped out and ran away. The rich man came and told them to leave because of their unfaithfulness. The cause is not Adam or Eve. It is that humans are unable to live without God.

A child depends on their parents. People depend on God because He is our Father. He is the source of good. We need to be with God all the time. He is the primary source of all blessings in our lives.

## Lesson 2 The primary sin

> Read Romans 3:23, 6:23

Let us find out what sin is. What did Adam and Eve do? They disobeyed God. It was the first sin committed on Earth. But the primary sin was born in their hearts before that. It was rebellion (rejection of God’s laws). Rebellion caused disobedience to God.

Sin No. 1 and Sin No. 2 may look different — murders, lies, sexual immorality, quarrels, anger, envy — but all of them grow from one root: rebellion against God.

What are the main issues in relationships between parents and children? They are disobedience and rebellion.

The root of all sin is not murder or theft. These are the results of the primary sin. Adam and Eve doubted (rebelled against) God’s words. Rebellion gave birth to disobedience, upon which all other sins and wickedness people commit are based.

How can we get rid of the primary sin? You have to say no to your ego. In other words, you must give up the right to rule your life and give this right to God, the Creator of life.

And what is the primary source of blessings? It is giving your life to Jesus and obeying Him.

To get rid of bad fruit (sins) in your life, you have to fight their roots. You need to cut them off. Ask God to plant a seed of a new life. Do it right now.

Love. Health. Prosperity. Peace. Success. Joy.

Jesus is my Lord.

Pray together with me:

“Dear Jesus. I give up my rebellion and disobedience. I give all my life to you. Be my Lord (Master), change me, and teach me to obey you, my Lord. Amen”.

## Lesson 3 The father of sin

> Read Matthew 13:24-30, 36-43

When reading this passage of the Scripture, we see that there is someone behind all evil. He is a fallen angel. God banished him from Heaven for his pride. He has many names in the Bible, two of which are the most common. The first one, “Devil,” means accuser, and “Satan” means enemy. Other names are “Old Serpent,” “Father of Lies,” “Liar,” “Dragon,” “Antichrist,” and others. All these names reveal the nature of the Devil.

Recently, I read in a newspaper about two criminals caught by police. They were husband and wife and wanted to steal money from a rich man. They tried different ways, but everything failed. They couldn’t do it. Then, they kidnapped his son, and demanded a $70,000 ransom. They found his one weakness: his child. The wealthy man immediately agreed to give them the money, but fortunately, the police quickly arrested the criminals. To attack God, the Devil has found His most precious creation: you and I and all the other people in the world.

People are more precious to God than anything else on Heaven and Earth. God gave His Son, Jesus Christ, for us.

The Devil pursues people to hurt God. He is a sly and cunning enemy because he knows God loves people the most.

Many years ago, there was a conflict between the USSR and China. The Chinese came up with a trick. They formed a rank of women holding their little children and placed them in front of their army. There were many soldiers and tanks behind the human shield. No one knew what to do. No one wanted to kill women and children. Then, one officer got the idea to put mines in the field and put up signs in Chinese that read: “Women, stop. Do not kill your children!” When the Chinese women read the signs, they stopped and did not move further into the minefield.

That is similar to what the Devil does. He does not attack us directly. He tempts a person close to us, incites us against each other, and gleefully watches from afar.

Stop. Your brother is not your enemy. The Devil set this up.

## Lesson 4 The consequences of sin

> Read James 1:13-15

Many laws exist in both the spiritual and the physical worlds. The effects and consequences of these laws do not depend on the people's authority. They work because they are laws. Let us consider one important law called “The law of sin and death.” Keep your Bible open. We will read James 1:13-15 one more time.

Desire (that does not come from God) gives birth to sin. Sin (rebellion, disobedience) gives birth to death.

Let us define this law in our own words: my plans, intentions, and desires will surely lead to disagreement with God, which will result in the complete failure of my life.

I spoke about this law with a man who said, “I do not believe this. Everything’s fine with me. I plan my life by myself.” I told him, “There is the law of gravity. Imagine that you do not believe it. You can stand on the top floor, shout, ‘I don’t believe this law!’, and bravely step forward. What is going to happen? You will fall to death. The point is that this law works regardless of whether you believe it. It operates beyond the human sphere of influence.”

The Bible says that desire gives birth, and sin gives birth. What does it mean? It means it is a process that may occur the next day or in a decade. A woman does not give birth in a single moment. It is a process that takes 9 months. Likewise, sooner or later, desire will give birth to sin, and sin will give birth to death. It will definitely happen.

Desire:
To live without God, do what I want, pleasure, I am my own master, no one can tell me what to do.

Sin:
Disobedience, quarrels, envy, murder, theft, fornication, drunkenness.

Death:
Disease, failure, depression, suffering in Hell.

You might ask if it is possible to stop this law from taking effect. No, it is not possible for a human. What to do then? Look what God has done through Jesus Christ. Isaiah 53:4-6 says:

“Surely he took up our pain and bore our suffering, yet we considered him punished by God, stricken by him, and afflicted. But he was pierced for our transgressions, he was crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was on him, and by his wounds we are healed. We all, like sheep, have gone astray, each of us has turned to our own way; and the Lord has laid on him the iniquity of us all.”

I give my life to Jesus. Jesus bore my sin and its consequences. Life: peace, joy, prosperity, eternal life.

This is the only way. Jesus took up my sin and all its consequences (He suffered and died) and has risen from the dead because He was the righteous Son of God.

## Lesson 5 There is no other way

> Read John 6:28-29

People who have met Jesus on Earth have asked Him, “What must we do to do the works God requires?” Jesus answered that there are no such works but one, and it is to believe in Him.

“Then,” you may say, “why help the poor, go to church, do good things, and live honestly?” We need to do good things (we’ll talk about this later), but they have nothing to do with our salvation. It does not matter if a person is good or bad. In any case, there is no salvation without Christ. To receive salvation, someone must pay and accept punishment for sin, which is death.

Once, I met a very nice man. He was very hospitable, kind, respectful, and even a little shy. He really wanted to share a meal with me, and during our conversation, he mentioned that he had “recently returned.” Later, I learned his story from other people. One night, he was driving a motorcycle and accidentally hit his neighbor, who fell and broke his arm. There was a court hearing, and he was sentenced to two years in a minimum security prison. I was surprised that such a good man had been put in prison. There are so many criminals walking the streets, and this man had to go to prison because of an accident. But, my dear friend, the law does not care if you are good or bad. If you broke the law, you must pay for it.

Do you see the point? If I have committed a single sin in my life, I am guilty. Only someone who will agree to take up my guilt and go to prison for me can save me.

And He is Jesus Christ. He died for me, was punished for me, set me free, and rose from the dead (walked out of prison). He is the Lord.

That’s why there is no other way but through Jesus Christ. Do not try to become good and then come to God. We cannot become good without Him. Do not try to receive salvation through knowledge or by going to church (although we need to do it). Salvation is possible only through Jesus Christ. The Devil has set up so many traps regarding this: “I will start in the New Year,” “I will live for myself just a bit, and then…”, “I will raise my children and then come to God,” “I will build my house first,” “I will get married and repent.” All these things are lies.

There is only one day when you can repent and give your life to Jesus, which is today.
