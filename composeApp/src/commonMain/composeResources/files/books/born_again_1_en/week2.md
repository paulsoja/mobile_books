# Week 2 Subject: The cause of all problems is sin

## Lesson 1 How it all happened

> Read Chapters 1-3 of the book of Genesis

Today, we begin a new topic: The cause of all problems is sin. I believe you have noticed several things after reading the first three chapters of Genesis:

1) God created everything.
2) Adam and Eve were at the center of creation.
3) The Serpent (Devil) deceived people.

Let us review, step by step.

God created everything good. He did not plan sin, disease, famine, or war. Humans were His most important creation. Everything was created for them: Earth, the Universe, plants, and animals. What made humans different from animals, in your opinion? Was it their intellect, strength, or memory? It was more than that. Humans were created in God’s image and likeness and had the right to choose.

When Adam and Eve were given a choice (God explained everything to Adam): whether to have joy, happiness, prosperity, health, and eternity in obedience to God or to be independent and face death, disease, and poverty, they chose the latter option of independence. And along with that independence, they got all the problems we experience now. When I was a child, I used to think that if I were Adam, I would have chosen God. No! In the position of Adam and Eve, we all would have done the same.

There is an old story about a newlywed couple and a rich man. The couple grumbled about Adam and Eve. They said their lives would have been different if Adam and Eve had not done what they did. One rich Christian heard about this and met this newly married couple. He said he would provide them with everything they needed: a house, furniture, and money, but under one condition. There will be two plates on their table, one flipped on top of the other, and they are not allowed to open them. If they do it even once, they will lose everything they received from him. They were patient for a couple of days, but then, when the husband was not at home, the wife lifted the top plate a little, and suddenly, a mouse jumped out and ran away. The rich man came and told them to leave because of their unfaithfulness.

The cause is not Adam or Eve. It is that humans are unable to live without God.

A child depends on their parents. People depend on God because He is our Father. He is the source of good. We need to be with God all the time. He is the primary source of all blessings in our lives!

## Lesson 2 The primary sin

> Read Romans 3:23, 6:23

Let us find out what sin is. What did Adam and Eve do? They disobeyed God. It was the first sin committed on Earth. But the primary sin was born in their hearts before that. It was <b>rebellion</b> (rejection of God’s laws). Rebellion caused disobedience to God.

What are the main issues in relationships between parents and children? They are disobedience and rebellion.

The root of all sin is not murder or theft. These are the results of the primary sin. Adam and Eve doubted (rebelled against) God’s words. Rebellion gave birth to disobedience, upon which all other sins and wickedness people commit are based.

How can we get rid of the primary sin? You have to say no to your ego. In other words, you must give up the right to rule your life and give this right to God, the Creator of life.

And what is the primary source of blessings? It is giving your life to Jesus and obeying Him.

To get rid of bad fruit (sins) in your life, you have to fight their roots! You need to cut them off! Ask God to plant a seed of a new life! Do it right now!

<b>JESUS IS MY LORD</b>

Pray together:

“Dear Jesus. I give up my rebellion and disobedience. I give all my life to you. Be my Lord (Master), change me, and teach me to obey you, my Lord. Amen”.

## Lesson 3 The father of sin

> Read Matthew 13:24-30, 36-43

When reading this passage of the Scripture, we see that there is someone behind all evil. He is a fallen angel. God banished him from Heaven for his pride. He has many names in the Bible.

The first one, <b>Devil</b>, means accuser, and <b>Satan</b> means enemy. Other names are “Old Serpent,” “Father of Lies,” “Liar,” “Dragon,” “Antichrist,” and others. All these names reveal the nature of the Devil.

Recently, I read in a newspaper about two criminals caught by police. They were husband and wife and wanted to steal money from a rich man. They tried different ways, but everything failed. Then they kidnapped his son and demanded ransom. They found his one weakness: his child.

To attack God, the Devil has found His most precious creation: you and I.

People are more precious to God than anything else on Heaven and Earth. God gave His Son, Jesus Christ, for us.

The Devil pursues people to hurt God. He is a sly and cunning enemy because he knows God loves people the most.

That is similar to what the Devil does. He does not attack us directly. He tempts a person close to us, incites us against each other, and gleefully watches from afar.

<b>Stop! Your brother is not your enemy. The Devil set this up!</b>

## Lesson 4 The consequences of sin

> Read James 1:13-15

Many laws exist in both the spiritual and the physical worlds. Let us consider one important law called <b>the law of sin and death</b>.

Desire gives birth to sin, and sin gives birth to death.

Let us define this law in our own words: my plans, intentions, and desires will surely lead to disagreement with God, which will result in the complete failure of my life.

This law works regardless of whether you believe it.

Sooner or later, desire will give birth to sin, and sin will give birth to death. It will definitely happen.

You might ask if it is possible to stop this law. No, it is not possible for a human.

Look what God has done through Jesus Christ:

“He took up our pain and bore our suffering... the Lord has laid on him the iniquity of us all.”

<b>This is the only way!</b>

Jesus took up my sin and all its consequences and rose from the dead.

> “Salvation is found in no one else...”

Who can help us avoid the consequences?

<b>Jesus</b>

## Lesson 5 There is no other way

> Read John 6:28-29

People asked Jesus what they must do. He answered: there is only one thing — to believe in Him.

We need to do good things, but they have nothing to do with salvation. There is no salvation without Christ.

If I have committed even one sin, I am guilty. Only someone who takes my punishment can save me.

<b>And that is Jesus Christ!</b>

He died for me, was punished for me, set me free, and rose from the dead.

That’s why there is no other way but through Jesus Christ.

The Devil sets traps:

“I will start later...”  
“I will do it after...”  
“I will live first...”

All these things are lies.

<b>There is only one day to repent:</b>

<b>TODAY!</b>
