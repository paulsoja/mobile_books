# Week 10 Subject: Go and do not stop

## Lesson 1 The Great Commission

> Read Mark. 16:15-20

I recall a story told to me by a well-known preacher and evangelist. He served as a missionary in Siberia. There was a car accident, and a young woman was severely injured. There were no other cars close to the site of the accident, so this brother had to take this injured woman to a hospital, which was dozens of kilometers away. The whole time, the woman pleaded, “I want to live, I really want to live.” He did not understand the real meaning of her words and drove faster and faster. She died when they almost arrived at the hospital. Suddenly, he realized everything… God sent him to tell the woman about salvation. Christ did not want her to go to Hell and gave her a chance by sending this brother to her. Unfortunately, he did not say anything. Due to our silence and shyness, thousands and millions of people, young and old, go to Hell for eternal suffering. God does everything in His power for us to meet those people, but very often, instead of witnessing life in Jesus Christ to them, we keep silent or talk about meaningless things. The eternal death of this young woman weighs on the missionary’s conscience. How many lost people weigh on our conscience? May God help us to answer this question.
In verse 15, Jesus spoke about His most important Commandment, also known as “the Great Commission.” Let us review this Commission:

center: <b>Go</b>
Jesus did not tell us that people must come to us. We must go to them. Where do we have to go? Everywhere we live, work, travel, or serve as missionaries. Christ commissioned His Church to tell the Good News to the whole world!


center: <b>Preach</b>
Knowing what to preach is crucial. The Bible does not tell us to preach about a church, a good pastor, or a choir. It also does not tell us to show which church or denominational branches are better and so on. Jesus did not ask us to judge. He sent us to preach the Gospel. What do we need to tell people? Read Lesson 1 of Week 3. This is the Gospel: the salvation of people from sin, diseases, suffering, and eternal Hell through the Only Savior Jesus Christ!

center: <b>Confirmation</b>
In verse 20, it is said that the disciples <u>went out and preached</u>, and then God began to confirm His Word with great blessings! Many people were freed from demons, and many were healed of illnesses. There was supernatural wisdom in sermons and God’s powerful protection.
May the Lord help you fulfill His Great Commission at every time and place.

## Lesson 2 Serving others as Jesus did


> Read Mark. 5:13-16

When we read the New Testament carefully, we can see that Jesus Christ not only preached but also helped the poor, healed, taught, and so on. Jesus was the King, but he often took on the role of a servant, doctor, advisor, and mentor. Let us review the big picture of the Gospel.

center: <b>Four stages of evangelism</b>

1. <b>Prayer</b>. We can not save anyone by our own efforts. Only God saves. Almost every person is controlled by demons, and their influence must be destroyed with the name of Jesus. This stage resembles <u>plowing</u>.
2. <b>Good deeds</b>. You need to show Jesus in action. People want to see change in our words and knowledge, but in our lives and deeds. We can compare this stage to <u>fertilization</u>.
3. <b>Preaching</b>. After praying and “fertilizing,” we need to talk about Jesus. I want to remind you that you need to tell people about Jesus, not about a denomination, debated topics, or a pastor. Only Jesus Christ gives salvation! Preaching resembles <u>sowing</u>.
4. <b>Discipleship</b>. After a person accepts Jesus as their Lord, we need to train them in God’s truth. The most effective way is discipleship. Discipleship can be compared to <u>weeding and watering</u>.

A non-believing family lived close to us. My wife and I prayed about their salvation for a long time. When we noticed a slight interest in us and God on their side, we began to help this family, even though they were wealthier. It touched them, and a short time later, the woman agreed to go to a church service. We continued to pray and help them in various ways. A while later, her husband came to "see where his wife is going.” The Lord touched their hearts, and they repented. My wife became a mentor for this family, which helped them grow spiritually, and soon, they started preparing to serve in the church.
Only a patient farmer will reap a good harvest. Seeds can be lost if sowed into unprepared soil or not cultivated and watered. Many of us have gardens and take care of them. People are so much more precious! They are in dire need of cultivation, weeding, and watering.

## Lesson 3 Fulfilling the Great Commission


> Read Rom. 1:16-17

The Devil puts many obstacles in the way of the Gospel. One of the biggest and most widespread ones is excessive shyness. Destroy this obstacle with the power of the Lord Jesus!

1. <b>Your social circle</b>. It includes your family, neighbors, co-workers or classmates, relatives, and friends.

2. <b>Chance meetings</b>. On our way to work, a store, or on vacation, we meet hundreds of people. They need salvation, too! Pray, and God will show you who and when you can share the Gospel with. Always keep an invitation to the church, home church, and a copy of the New Testament with you. Pay attention: God will show you the needs of people, and you will be able to serve them.
3. <b>Special</b> evangelism meetings. Jesus preached not only to those people who came to Him. In most cases, He brought the Good News to the people. Organize such meetings in your home church, with your mentor, or with other brothers and sisters. You must pray for God’s revelations and His protection before the meeting. Do not be afraid. Be brave, and the Lord will use you in His great work of saving people!

<b>✔ Remember: no mission is more important, no goal is higher, and no ministry is greater than saving people.</b>
Evangelism brings great joy. But this is not everything. God promised a big reward in Heaven and a hundredfold blessing on Earth.

## Lesson 4 Dedication

> Read Rom. 12:1-2

Does the word “sacrifice” scare you? Apostle Paul does not write about some blood sacrifices here. He writes about complete dedication to ministry. In the New Testament, there is only one sacrifice in the literal sense: Jesus’ death on the cross!
Dedication means separation for a special purpose. There can be many blank notebooks on your bookshelf. They are not “dedicated” because they are not separated for a particular purpose. If you take a notebook and write “Prayer diary” or “Sunday sermons” on the cover and use it, then this notebook is separated or “dedicated” for a particular purpose.
People also dedicate their lives to different purposes, sometimes without even realizing it. How many people dedicate themselves to pumping alcohol through their bodies? How many think only about fornication, money, or other vain things? Many people dedicate their lives to widening their knowledge, receiving academic degrees, and publishing their works. Some completely gave themselves away to digital devices or the TV. There are adventurers who seek pirate treasure and gold veins their whole life. There are also many people who spend their lives in misery, not living but merely existing.
What did Jesus Christ dedicate His life to? It was for the salvation of you, me, and all people. What an honorable and noble purpose! There is no creation in the world more valuable than people and no purpose higher than their salvation.
Once, I spoke with a young woman. She was not opposed to the Gospel, but first, she wanted to have some more “fun” in life. When we began to talk about accepting Jesus as the Lord, the woman said, “I would like to, but not now. When I retire, I will repent right away!” I answered, “Would you want your husband or fiancé to answer you in that way, “I’m fine with becoming your husband, but I’d like to have more fun first, and when I retire, I’ll return to you, without teeth, and with a stick in my hand!”. This young woman repented and began to serve the Lord.
What is your decision? What are you going to spend your time, health, and life on?
Do you want to join the millions-strong army of Christians for the salvation of people?
