# Week 10 Subject: Go and do not stop

## Lesson 1 The Great Commission

> Read Matthew 28:18-20

Jesus gave a mission to every believer:
- go
- preach
- make disciples

This is not optional — it is our purpose.

---

## Lesson 2 Serving others as Jesus did

> Read Mark 10:45

Jesus came to serve, not to be served.

We are called to:
- help others  
- care  
- serve with love  

---

## Lesson 3 Fulfilling the Great Commission

> Read Acts 1:8

“You will be my witnesses…”

We can share faith:
- with words  
- with actions  
- with lifestyle  

---

## Lesson 4 Dedication

> Read Romans 12:1

Offer your life to God.

Dedication means:
- surrender  
- commitment  
- obedience  

---

## Lesson 5 What is next?

> Read Philippians 3:13-14

Christian life is a journey.

Do not stop:
- keep growing  
- keep learning  
- keep moving forward
